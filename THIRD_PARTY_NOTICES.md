# Third-party materials and provenance status

The original development archive includes a trained model artifact (`stemscore_core.joblib`) and a microscopy reference library. They are excluded from this source snapshot because their provenance and redistribution permissions have not yet been established.

The model metadata names GEO accessions GSE304806 and GSE314132 and reports 89 training samples. The exact sample selection, label mapping, preprocessing and model-training script have not yet been independently reconstructed.

Before redistributing any trained model, expression matrix, sample annotation or microscopy image, verify the original source terms, submitter/publisher conditions and any applicable third-party licences. Public availability of a dataset does not by itself establish permission to redistribute every derived artifact.
